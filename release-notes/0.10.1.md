Schlift now trains you for muscle growth with workouts you compose.

• Training programs (Linear 5x5, GZCLP, 5/3/1) are gone. Build a workout from any exercises, or save templates and let the app suggest one.
• The app prescribes sets, reps, rest and weight for every exercise and progresses each on its own.
• A weekly volume tracker shows sets per muscle.
• Your working weights carried over. GZCLP lifters kept the heavier of their two tiers.
• New home screen, template editor and tutorial.
