- Skip on the warmup row skips one warmup at a time, not all of them.
- The weight sheet in a workout has a Sets stepper: add sets to an exercise or take away ones you haven't done.
- The workout's progress bar reaches the end when you finish, even if you skipped warmups.
- The rest countdown bar runs out exactly when the rest does.
- A Motivation button in a workout shows that workout's motivational quote; a finished workout shows the one it was done under.
- Every page opened from the menu has just a title and a back arrow; Log and Heart open as pages with the workout's status still showing underneath.
- Text is no longer written in all capitals.
- One typeface for words throughout; the mono face is kept for numbers and clocks.
- The time estimate no longer learns from sets tapped through in a second or two.
- A new look: no banner on the workout, sections that run the full width, rounded buttons and boxes, and a workout screen that fits more on a small phone.
- Lifting is green, on the phone and the watches.
- A new app icon: a barbell's plates.
- Multiplayer is a button at the top of home and the workout; heart rate, time and the log open from boxes above your status.
- Editing a ready-made workout says Edit, and makes it yours when you save.
- Your workout shows as a live status while the app is out of sight, with its clock: on Android in your notifications, lock screen and status bar, on iPhone on the lock screen and in the Dynamic Island.
- The website lists live status and motivation, and its pictures and the store screenshots show the new look.
